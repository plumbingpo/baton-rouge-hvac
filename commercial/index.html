Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Baton Rouge HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your operating hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.
