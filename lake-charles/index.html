Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Lake Charles

A little routine care is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement.
